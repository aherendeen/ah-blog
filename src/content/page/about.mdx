## Hey, I'm Andrew.

I build, tinker with, and explore things around software, self-hosting, automation, AI, and the web.

This site is my place to collect what I'm working on, document things I want to remember, and share ideas, tools, projects, and discoveries that might be useful to someone else.

## What you'll find here

- **Projects** — things I'm building, experimenting with, or maintaining.
- **Notes** — solutions, references, and lessons worth keeping around.
- **Guides** — practical walkthroughs for tools and setups I've spent time with.
- **Experiments** — new technologies, workflows, and ideas I'm testing.
- **Recommendations** — software, services, and projects I think are worth knowing about.

I tend to gravitate toward tools that are useful, composable, and give you more control over how things work.

## This site

The site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and kept intentionally lightweight.

- [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com) for styling
- [Pagefind](https://pagefind.app) for search
- [Giscus](https://giscus.app) for discussions
- [RSS](/rss.xml) for following new posts

The source is available on GitHub if you want to look around, borrow an idea, or see how something works.
